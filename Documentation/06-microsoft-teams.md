## 7. Key Concepts Learned

### Team

A workspace for a group of users working together.

### Channel

A section inside a Team used to organize communication and work.

### Standard Channel

Available to all members of the Team.

### Private Channel

Available only to selected Team members.

### Owner

Has management permissions for the Team.

### Member

Regular user of the Team.

### Teams Policy

Controls Teams features and capabilities available to users at the organization or assigned-policy level.

## 8. Enterprise Scenario

Example structure:

- `Helpdesk` → support requests

This structure demonstrates practical use of Teams for IT operations.
